How to play Cube Push

The board is a 5×5 grid of cubes that start blank. On your turn, take any cube on the outer edge that is blank or already shows your symbol (never your opponent's), turn it to your symbol, and push it back into the board from one of the other ends of its row or column. The cubes in that line slide along by one to make room. You can't put the cube back where you took it from. Make five of your symbol in a row, column or diagonal to win. Be careful: if your push lines up five of your opponent's symbol, they win, even if you made five of your own at the same time.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a glowing edge cube, then tap one of the yellow arrows (or the highlighted cube at the end of the line) to push it in. Tap the cube again to cancel.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click the same way, or move with the arrow keys, press Enter to pick a cube, then press the arrow key for the direction to push. Esc cancels.

Tips

Corner cubes have only two ways back in, while edge cubes have three. Every push also shifts your opponent's cubes, so a line that looks safe can break apart or come together in one move. Watch for pushes that would slide four of your opponent's cubes into a row of five. Hard mode looks several moves ahead and never misses a winning push.
