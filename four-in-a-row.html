How to play Four in a Row

The board has 7 columns and 6 rows. You play Red and go first; the computer plays Yellow. Pick a column and your disc drops to the lowest empty space. Get four of your discs in a line across, up and down, or diagonally to win. Block your opponent when they have three lined up. If the board fills with no four in a row, the game is a draw.

Controls

  • Phone: tap anywhere in a column to drop a disc there.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click a column, or move the disc with ← → and press Enter or Space. Number keys 1–7 drop straight into a column.

Tips

The middle column is the strongest: it is part of the most possible lines. Try to build two threats at once so your opponent can only block one. Watch the spaces just above a threat, because filling the space below hands it to the other player. Hard mode never misses a win or a block, so plan ahead.
