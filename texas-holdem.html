How to play Texas Hold'em

Everyone starts with 1,000 chips. Each hand the dealer button moves one seat to the left, the two players after it post the small and big blinds, and every player gets two private hole cards. There are four betting rounds: before the flop, after the flop (three shared cards), after the turn (a fourth) and after the river (a fifth). On your turn you can fold, check when there is nothing to call, call, or bet and raise any amount up to all your chips; a raise must be at least as big as the last bet or raise. Players who go all-in can only win the chips they matched, so extra bets go into side pots. At the showdown the best five-card hand from your two cards and the five on the table wins, and equal hands split the pot. Blinds rise every 6 hands; you win the game by taking every chip and lose when your stack runs out. Chips are just for fun and never worth anything.

Controls

  • Turn clock: every player has 60 seconds per move. When it runs out you automatically check if you can, otherwise fold.
  • Phone: tap Fold, Check/Call or Raise at the bottom. Drag the slider or tap Min, ½ Pot, Pot or All-in to choose your raise, then tap Raise.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click the buttons, or press F to fold, C or Space to check/call, R or Enter to raise, A to set all-in and the arrow keys to change the amount. N or Enter starts the next hand.
  • Pass & Play: pick 2 to 6 players. Before each turn a cover asks to pass the device; the next player taps (or presses Enter) to see their cards, and they are hidden again after the move.

Tips

Play fewer, stronger hands from early seats and open up on the button, where you act last after the flop. Compare the price of a call with the pot: calling 50 into a 150 pot only needs you to win a quarter of the time. The four opponents play differently: Rosa is tight and aggressive, Milo calls too much, Kenji bluffs a lot and Ada only bets with real hands, so adjust to each of them.
