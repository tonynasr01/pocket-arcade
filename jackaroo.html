How to play Jackaroo

Four players sit around the board in two teams, with partners facing each other. On Easy, Medium and Hard you (bottom) and your computer partner Noura (top) play Fahad and Saad. In 2 Players, Blue and Red are rivals and each gets a computer partner; in 4 Players, Blue and Green play Red and Orange. In both, a cover screen asks you to pass the device so nobody sees another player's cards. Each player has 4 marbles in their base, and a round deals 5 cards each, then 4 and 4 from the same deck. On your turn play one card to move a marble clockwise: A brings a marble out to your start square or moves 1 or 11, K brings one out or moves 13 and burns (sends home) every other marble it passes, Q moves 12, J swaps one of your marbles with a rival's, 10 moves 10 or makes the next player burn a random card, 7 can be split between two of your marbles, 5 moves any marble on the board 5, 4 moves back 4, and every other card moves its number. Landing on another marble sends it back to base, but a marble sitting on its own start square is protected and nobody can pass it. Marbles enter their four home squares with an exact count; if no card can be played you must burn one. Once your 4 marbles are home you play your partner's marbles, and the first team with all 8 marbles home wins.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a card at the bottom, then tap a glowing marble. Small buttons appear for A (1 or 11), 7 (how to split) and 10 (burn). For J or a split 7, tap your first marble and then the second one.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click cards and marbles, or press 1-5 to pick a card, arrow keys to choose a marble, Enter to play it and Esc to cancel.
  • Pass and play: when the 'Pass to …' screen appears, hand the device over; the next player taps (or presses Enter) to see their cards.

Tips

Keep an A or K for when marbles are stuck in base, and save the 4: backing up from your start square puts a marble right in front of your home. Leaving a marble on your start square blocks rivals, but also stops you bringing out another one. Don't park just in front of a rival's marble or on a rival's start square, use 5s to push your partner forward, and remember the King burns every marble in its path, including your partner's.
