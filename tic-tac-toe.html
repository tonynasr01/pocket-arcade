How to play Tic-Tac-Toe

Get three in a row before your opponent does. Play the computer, where Easy makes mistakes, Medium blocks your wins, and Hard plays perfectly, or pick 2 Players to take turns with a friend on the same device.

Controls

Tap a square to place your mark. Use the arrows at the top to choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or 2 Players.

Tips

Take the center or a corner on your first move.

Your best score is saved in your browser.
