How to play Tic-Tac-Toe

Get three in a row before the computer does. Easy mode makes mistakes, Medium blocks your wins, and Hard plays perfectly, so the best you can do there is a draw.

Controls

Tap a square to place your X. Switch difficulty with the arrows at the top.

Tips

Take the center or a corner on your first move.

Your best score and unlocked levels are saved in your browser, so you can pick up where you left off.
