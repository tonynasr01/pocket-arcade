How to play Mafia

Choose the number of players, type their names and pick the roles: 1 to 3 Mafia (1 for 4–5 players, 2 for 6–8, 3 for 9–12 by default), plus an optional Doctor and Detective; everyone else is a Villager. Pass the phone around so each player privately flips their role card; Mafia also see who their partners are. Every night the phone goes around the whole circle so nobody can tell who has a power: each Mafia member votes for a victim (most votes wins, a tie is settled at random), the Doctor protects one player but never the same player two nights in a row, the Detective learns whether one player is Mafia, and Villagers tap a suspect just to keep the secret. In the morning the narrator announces who died, or that the Doctor saved the victim. By day the town talks against a timer and then votes, either openly by show of hands or with a secret ballot passed around; the player with the most votes is eliminated, while a tie (or more skips than votes) means nobody goes. The town wins when all Mafia are gone, and the Mafia win as soon as they match the rest of the town in number.

Controls

  • Phone: tap the buttons; on each 'Pass to…' screen hand the phone over and the next player taps when ready. Tap your role card to flip it.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click the buttons, or press Enter or Space to open a pass screen and Enter to press the main button.

Tips

Players who die should stay quiet but can keep watching. As the Detective, think twice before revealing yourself: the Mafia will target you next. Mafia players should vote like everyone else and avoid defending each other too obviously. Your player names and role settings are saved in the browser for next time, and the narrator voice can be turned off on any public screen.
