How to play Checkers

You play Red and move first from the bottom of the board. Pieces move one square diagonally forward onto an empty dark square. Jump over an opponent's piece to capture it, and if you can jump you must. After a jump, keep jumping with the same piece while you can. A piece that reaches the far row is crowned a king and can then move and jump backwards too. You win when your opponent has no pieces left or cannot move.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a piece, then tap a glowing square. Pieces that must jump glow yellow.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click a piece and a square, or move the cursor with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space.

Tips

Keep your back row filled as long as you can so the other side can't crown kings easily. Trade pieces when you are ahead and keep your pieces together in the centre. Watch for double jumps: giving up one piece can set up a jump that takes two. Hard mode looks many moves ahead, so try Medium first.
