How to play Code Words

The board has 25 words. Secretly, 9 belong to the team that goes first, 8 to the other team, 7 are innocent bystanders and 1 is the assassin. On a team's turn its spymaster, who can see the key card, gives a one-word clue and a number, such as OCEAN 3. Teammates then guess words one at a time: a correct guess lets them keep going, up to the number plus one extra guess. Hitting a bystander or the other team's agent ends the turn, and the assassin loses the game on the spot. The first team to find all its agents wins. In Solo the computer is your spymaster and Blue finds one of its agents every turn, so race to find your 9 before Blue finds its 8.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a card to select it and tap it again to guess. Tap End turn to stop early. In Party, type the clue, pick the number with − and +, and tap Give clue.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click cards (twice to guess), or move with the arrow keys and press Enter twice; E ends the turn.
  • Party: tap Spymaster view and pass the device to the spymaster, or let spymasters open Code Words on their own phones, choose Spymaster key and type the 4-character game code to see the same key card.

Tips

In Solo every clue names a theme, and the number tells you how many of your agents fit it. Words from other themes are traps, so if a card only half fits, save it for later. The extra guess is handy for picking up an agent you missed from an earlier clue. Your fastest winning time is saved in your browser.
