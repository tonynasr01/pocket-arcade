How to play Solitaire

Build four piles, one per suit, from Ace up to King. In the seven columns, stack cards in descending order with alternating colors, red on black or black on red. Only a King can go in an empty column. Tap the deck to draw new cards. When it runs out, tap again to turn the used cards back over.

Controls

  • Tap a card to move it to the best available spot.
  • Tap the deck to draw.
  • ↶ Undo takes back your last move.
  • Pick Draw 1 or Draw 3 from the menu at the top.

Tips

Turn over face-down cards in the columns whenever you can. Once every card is face up, the game finishes itself.
