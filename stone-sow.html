How to play Stone Sow

The 4×4 board starts with two neutral stones on each corner, and each player has 8 stones of their colour. On your turn put one of your stones on top of any stack, then pick up that whole stack and sow it: drop the stones one at a time on neighbouring squares (up, down, left or right), starting with the bottom stone, so your own stone lands last. You choose the path square by square; you may never step straight back to the square you just left, but you can loop around and visit a square again. Only the top stone of each stack counts. Line up four of your colour on top in a row, column or diagonal to win. If a move makes lines for both colours, the player who moved wins; if it only makes a line for the other colour, the other player wins. If all 16 stones are played with no line, the game is a draw.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a stack to pick it up, then tap the glowing squares one at a time to drop each stone. Tap Undo to take back the last drop.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click a stack and then the squares, or move with the arrow keys, press Enter to pick up a stack, use the arrows to sow and Backspace to undo.

Tips

Your own stone always lands on the last square of the path, so plan where the path ends. Long stacks let you cover lots of squares and bury your opponent's stones under neutral or your own ones. Before you finish a move, check that you have not uncovered a line for the other colour. Hard mode looks at every reply you could make, so it rarely leaves you a winning move.
