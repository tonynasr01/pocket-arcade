How to play Cage Sums

Fill an N by N grid with the numbers 1 to N so that no number appears twice in any row or column. The grid is split into cages with thick outlines. Each cage shows a target and a sign: the numbers in a + cage add up to the target, × cages multiply to it, and a − or ÷ cage has two squares whose difference or quotient (bigger by smaller) is the target. A cage with just a number is already given. Numbers may repeat inside a cage as long as they are not in the same row or column. Every puzzle is freshly generated and has exactly one solution.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a square, then tap a number on the pad. ⌫ erases. Turn on ✏️ Notes to pencil in several options; 💡 Hint fills a square and ✔ Check marks wrong entries.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click a square or move with the arrow keys, type a number, Backspace or 0 erases. N toggles notes, C checks, H gives a hint, E toggles conflict highlighting.

Tips

Start with the given single squares and with cages that allow only one combination, such as 3÷ in a 4×4 (only 1 and 3) or a two-square 7+ in a 4×4 (3 and 4). In every row the numbers always add up to the same total (10 for 4×4, 15 for 5×5), which can pin down the last square of a cage. Use notes for pairs you know but can't place yet. The timer starts when the puzzle appears, and your best time for each size, without hints, is saved in the browser.
