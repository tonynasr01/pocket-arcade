How to play Pento Box

Each puzzle gives you a box five squares tall and a set of pieces that fill it exactly. Drag a piece onto the box and it snaps into place when it fits. Tap a piece to turn it a quarter turn, and use Flip to mirror it. Drag a piece off the box to put it back in the tray. The puzzle is solved when every square of the box is covered, and every set has at least one solution.

Controls

  • Phone: drag pieces with your finger, tap a piece to turn it and tap ⇋ Flip to mirror the last piece you touched.
  • Keyboard/mouse: drag with the mouse, click to turn, right-click to flip, or press R and F for the selected piece and N for the next puzzle.

Tips

Start with the awkward pieces such as the cross and the W, and fill the corners and edges first: the long straight piece and the L love the walls. If a single square is left cut off, no piece can ever fill it, so move things around early. Your best time for each size is saved in this browser.
