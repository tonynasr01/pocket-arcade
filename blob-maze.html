How to play Blob Maze

Guide the blob through the maze to eat every dot while avoiding the spiky enemies. Eat a gem and the enemies get scared for a few seconds, so you can eat them for bonus points. Side tunnels wrap around the screen.

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD on a computer. Swipe, or use the wheel or arrows on a phone. Gems let you eat the spikers.

Tips

Save gems for when enemies are close.

Your best score is saved in your browser.
