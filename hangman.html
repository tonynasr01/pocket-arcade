How to play Hangman

Guess the hidden word one letter at a time before six wrong guesses complete the drawing. Pick a word length from 4 letters up to 8 or more. To play with a friend, tap 2 players: one person types a secret word, and the other tries to guess it.

Controls

Tap letters or type on your keyboard. Tap 2 players to enter a secret word for a friend.

Tips

Start with common vowels and letters like r, s, t and n.
