How to play Fuse Jumper

Every level starts with 24 bombs to collect, and later levels add more, up to 40. One bomb always glows red with a lit fuse: grab it for double points, and the next nearest bomb lights up. Collecting the lit bombs in order earns a big bonus at the end of the level, up to 50,000 points if you miss no more than four. New enemies drift down slowly and can't hurt you for their first 3 seconds, then mummies walk the platforms and turn into birds that chase you. Touching one costs a life, and you have three. The levels never end, each with a new layout and more enemies.

Controls

  • Phone: move with the wheel or arrow buttons, tap the big ⤒ button to jump and ⤓ to drop down.
  • Keyboard: ← → to move, ↑ or Space to jump.
  • Hold jump to leap higher. Tap jump again while rising to stop the jump where you are, and hold it while falling to glide down slowly.
  • Press ↓ (or the ⤓ button on a phone) to drop down faster, or to fall straight through the platform you are standing on.

Tips

Plan a route that follows the lit fuse. Grab the P ball to turn every enemy into a coin you can collect for growing points, the B ball to raise your score multiplier, and the ❤ ball for an extra life (you can hold at most 3). Bonus balls never appear in the first seconds of a level and only one is ever on screen. Each level has one B ball, one P ball with a 50% chance of a second, and a 50% chance of a ❤. They disappear after 10 seconds, so grab them quickly. Gliding is the safest way to slip past birds. Your best score is saved in your browser.
