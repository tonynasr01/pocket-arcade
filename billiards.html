How to play Billiards

Break the rack, then the first ball you pot decides your group: solids (1 to 7) or stripes (9 to 15). Keep shooting as long as you pot your own balls. When your group is cleared, pot the 8 ball to win. Scratching, missing every ball or hitting the other group first is a foul, and your opponent gets ball in hand to place the cue ball anywhere. Potting the 8 early, or scratching on it, loses the game.

Controls

  • Phone: touch and drag on the table to aim, then pull the power bar on the right down and let go to shoot. Drag the cue ball to place it when you have ball in hand.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click and drag on the table to aim, drag the power bar to shoot. Arrow keys fine-tune the aim (Shift for bigger steps), Up/Down set power and Space shoots.

Tips

Follow the aim line: the ghost ball shows where the cue ball will hit and the short line shows where the object ball will go, turning red if you are about to hit the wrong ball. Softer shots are easier to control and leave the cue ball close to the rest of your group. Easy mode misses often; Hard mode rarely does, so leave it awkward shots when you cannot pot.
