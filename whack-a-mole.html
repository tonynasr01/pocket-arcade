How to play Whack-a-Mole

Moles pop out of holes for a moment. Whack each one before it hides. Every mole you miss costs a life, and after three the game is over. The moles keep getting faster, bombs start appearing after a while, and hitting a bomb also costs a life. A gold ⭐ is worth 3 points.

Controls

Tap moles while they are up. Leave the bombs alone.

Tips

Keep your eyes on the center hole and react outward.
