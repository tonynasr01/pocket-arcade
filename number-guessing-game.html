How to play Number Guess

The computer picks a secret number and tells you whether each guess is too high or too low. The colored bar shrinks to show where the number can still be. Each level widens the range, up to 1,000.

Controls

Type a guess and press Enter. The bar shows where the number can still be.

Tips

Always guess the middle of the remaining range.

Your best score and unlocked levels are saved in your browser, so you can pick up where you left off.
