How to play Bubble Pop

Bubbles bounce around the room. Shoot one with your harpoon and it splits into two smaller bubbles, and the smallest ones pop for good. Clear every bubble before the time bar at the bottom runs out, and don't let one touch you. Time left over is added as a bonus. Some levels are split by walls that open once one side is clear. The levels never end, with a bonus life every five levels.

Controls

  • Phone: move with the wheel or the arrow buttons and tap 🔱 to shoot.
  • Keyboard: ← → to move, Space to shoot.
  • Mouse: turn on 🖱️ Mouse under the game, then point to move and click to shoot.
  • 2 Players on a computer: turn on 👥 2 Players. Player 1 uses A, D and W. Player 2 uses ←, → and ↑.

Tips

Split big bubbles while they are high, then step aside as the pieces fall. Popped bubbles sometimes drop a power-up: ⚡ two harpoons at once, 📌 a sticky harpoon that stays at the ceiling, ⏱️ freezes time, 🛡️ blocks a hit, 💥 breaks every bubble down to the smallest size, ❤️ an extra life, and 🍒 bonus points.
