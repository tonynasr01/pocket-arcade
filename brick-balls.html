How to play Brick Balls

Drag to aim and let go to fire all your balls at once. Each hit takes one point off a brick, and a brick breaks when it reaches zero. After every shot the bricks move down one row and a new row appears. Collect the green +1 rings to add balls. The game ends when a brick reaches the floor.

Controls

  • Drag on the board to aim, then let go to shoot.
  • Keyboard: ← → to aim, Space to shoot.
  • Balls speed up the longer they bounce.

Tips

Bank shots off the side walls to reach bricks hiding behind others. Grab +1 rings early, since more balls make every later round easier.
