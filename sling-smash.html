How to play Sling Smash

Each level has a few Puffs waiting by the slingshot and one or more Grumblobs hiding in towers. Drag back and let go to launch; the dotted line shows how the flight starts. Real physics decides the rest: blocks topple, crack and break, and a Grumblob pops when it is hit hard enough or takes a big fall. Pop them all before you run out of Puffs to clear the level. Every Puff you have left over is worth a bonus and an extra star.

Controls

  • Phone: put your finger anywhere on the game, drag back and let go to launch. Tap the screen while a special Puff is in the air to use its power.
  • Keyboard/mouse: drag with the mouse and release, or use ↑ ↓ to aim, ← → to set the power and Space to launch. Space or a click in mid-air uses a special power.

Tips

Hit towers low to bring the whole thing down, and look for heavy stone blocks that can fall onto Grumblobs. The blue Trio splits into three and shatters ice, the yellow Zip speeds up and punches through wood, and the dark Boomer explodes, best right next to stone. Clear a level with Puffs to spare for three stars; your best stars and score for each level are saved in your browser.
