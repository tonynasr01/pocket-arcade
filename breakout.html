How to play Breakout

Bounce the ball off your paddle to break every brick. Some bricks need two or three hits. You have 3 lives. Pick a mode from Easy to Master, each with its own brick layout.

Controls

Move your finger or mouse to steer, or use the left and right keys.

Tips

Hit the ball with the paddle edge to aim it.
