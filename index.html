How to play

Pocket Arcade is a collection of free classic games that run directly in your web browser. There is nothing to download or install, and every game works with a touch screen, a mouse, or a keyboard. Here is a quick guide to each one.

2048

Slide all tiles in one direction. Matching numbers merge into one tile with double the value. Reach bigger tiles to clear each goal, from 128 upward. The game ends when no moves are left.

Tip: keep your biggest tile in one corner.

Backgammon

Each side has 15 checkers. You play White and move counter-clockwise toward your home board in the bottom right, while Red moves the opposite way. Roll two dice and move one checker by each number, or one checker by both; doubles are played four times, and you must use both dice when you can. A single checker on a point is a blot: land on it to hit it and send it to the bar, where it must re-enter in your opponent's home board before anything else moves. Once all 15 of your checkers are home you can bear them off, and the first side to bear off every checker wins.

Tip: avoid leaving blots where your opponent has many shots at them, and build points side by side to form a prime that traps their back checkers.

Billiards

Break the rack, then the first ball you pot decides your group: solids (1 to 7) or stripes (9 to 15). Keep shooting as long as you pot your own balls. When your group is cleared, pot the 8 ball to win. Scratching, missing every ball or hitting the other group first is a foul, and your opponent gets ball in hand to place the cue ball anywhere. Potting the 8 early, or scratching on it, loses the game.

Tip: follow the aim line: the ghost ball shows where the cue ball will hit and the short line shows where the object ball will go, turning red if you are about to hit the wrong ball.

Blob Maze

Guide the blob through the maze to eat every dot while avoiding the spiky enemies. Eat a gem and the enemies get scared for a few seconds, so you can eat them for bonus points. Side tunnels wrap around the screen.

Tip: save gems for when enemies are close.

Block Fill

Every board has one glowing start tile, some open tiles and a few blocked ones. Starting from the glowing tile, draw a path that moves up, down, left or right, one tile at a time. The path can never visit a tile twice and can't pass through blocked tiles. Fill every open tile to solve the puzzle. If you get stuck, drag back along your path to undo, or touch any filled tile to cut the path back to it.

Tip: tiles with only one open neighbor must be the last tile of your path, so plan to finish there.

Breakout

Bounce the ball off your paddle to break every brick. Some bricks need two or three hits. You have 3 lives, and each mode has its own brick layout.

Tip: hit the ball with the paddle edge to aim it.

Brick Balls

Drag to aim and fire a stream of balls at numbered bricks. Each hit lowers a brick by one. The bricks move down after every shot, and the game ends when one reaches the floor. Collect +1 rings for extra balls.

Tip: bank shots off the walls to hit bricks from the side.

Bubble Pop

Shoot bouncing bubbles to split them until they pop, before the time bar runs out. Levels never end, some are split by walls, and power-ups like the sticky harpoon and dynamite help out. Two players can share one keyboard.

Tip: split big bubbles while they are high above you.

Cannon Blast

Drag your cannon while it fires nonstop at bouncing rocks. Each rock splits in two when its number hits zero, and they keep getting tougher the longer you last. Grab the occasional power-up, and don't let a rock touch you.

Tip: keep moving and stay out from under falling rocks.

Checkers

You play Red and move first from the bottom of the board. Pieces move one square diagonally forward onto an empty dark square. Jump over an opponent's piece to capture it, and if you can jump you must. After a jump, keep jumping with the same piece while you can. A piece that reaches the far row is crowned a king and can then move and jump backwards too. You win when your opponent has no pieces left or cannot move.

Tip: keep your back row filled as long as you can so the other side can't crown kings easily.

Chess

Play White against the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard, or pick 2 Players to play a friend on one device. Castling, en passant and promotion are all included.

Tip: control the center and castle early.

Color Clash

Everyone gets 7 cards and one card is turned up to start the discard pile. On your turn, play a card that matches the top card's color, number or symbol. Skip makes the next player miss a turn, Reverse changes direction (with two players it works like a Skip), and Draw Two makes the next player draw 2 and miss a turn. A Wild lets you pick the new color, and a Wild +4 also makes the next player draw 4; in this version you may play it any time. If you can't or don't want to play, draw one card, and if it fits you can play it right away or keep it. When you play down to one card, tap Last card! before the next player moves or you draw 2 penalty cards. The first player with an empty hand wins.

Tip: hold on to your Wild cards for when you really need them, and save Draw Two and Skip cards for when the next player is close to winning.

Color Clash Flip

Every card has a light side and a dark side, and everyone plays the same side at once. Match the top card's color, number or symbol, or play a Wild and pick a color. On the light side, Draw One makes the next player draw 1 and miss a turn, Skip and Reverse work as usual, and Wild +2 makes them draw 2. Playing a Flip card turns over every hand, the deck and the discard pile, and play continues on the dark side with pink, teal, orange and purple cards. There, Draw Five gives the next player 5 cards, Skip Everyone lets you go again, and Wild Draw Color makes the next player draw until they get the color you picked. The backs of your opponents' cards show their other side, so you can see what they will hold after a flip. Tap Last card! when you play down to one card, and the first to empty their hand wins.

Tip: before you play a Flip, check what your hand looks like on the other side: the faces you see on the opponents' cards and the deck are what they'll get.

Color Connect

Each board has pairs of colored dots. Drag from one dot to its twin to draw a pipe through the squares in between. Pipes can only turn at right angles and can never cross each other. If you drag through another pipe, it gets cut back, and it grows again if you pull your pipe away during the same drag. A puzzle is solved when every pair is connected and no empty square is left.

Tip: start with pairs near the edges and corners, since they have the fewest ways to go.

Dots and Boxes

Players take turns drawing one line between two neighbouring dots. If your line closes the fourth side of a box, the box is yours: it fills with your color and you must draw another line. When every box has been claimed, the player with the most boxes wins. Easy and Medium play a 5×5 board, Hard uses a bigger 6×6 board and a computer that knows the endgame tricks, and 2 Players lets two people share one device.

Tip: avoid drawing the third side of a box, because that hands it to your opponent.

Flappy Bird

Tap to flap and keep the bird in the air. Fly through the gaps between pipes without touching them. Every 8 pipes the level goes up and the gaps get narrower.

Tip: use small, steady taps rather than big jumps.

Four in a Row

The board has 7 columns and 6 rows. You play Red and go first; the computer plays Yellow. Pick a column and your disc drops to the lowest empty space. Get four of your discs in a line across, up and down, or diagonally to win. Block your opponent when they have three lined up. If the board fills with no four in a row, the game is a draw.

Tip: the middle column is the strongest: it is part of the most possible lines.

Fuse Jumper

Leap and glide around the screen to collect every bomb, 24 at first and up to 40 on later levels. Grab the glowing lit bomb for double points, and follow the lit bombs in order for a big end-of-level bonus. Catch the ❤ ball for an extra life, up to 3. Dodge the mummies and birds, or grab the P ball to turn them into coins.

Tip: hold jump while falling to glide past enemies.

Hangman

Guess the hidden word one letter at a time before six wrong guesses complete the drawing. Words get longer on each level, or tap 2 players and type a secret word for a friend.

Tip: start with common vowels and letters like R, S, T and N.

Helix Drop

Spin the tower so the bouncing ball drops through the gaps in each ring. Red sections end your run. Fall through three rings in a row to smash the next one, and see how deep you can go. The tower never ends.

Tip: line up the next gap while the ball is still in the air.

Land Grab

Leave your land to draw a trail, then loop back to claim the trail and everything inside it. If a rival crosses your trail you are out, but you can knock them out the same way. Claim enough of the map to win.

Tip: short loops keep your trail safe.

Memory Match

All cards start face down. Flip two at a time to find matching pairs. Pick from 6 pairs up to 15. Fewer moves means a better score.

Tip: flip unknown cards first so you remember more positions.

Minesweeper

Dig squares to uncover the board. Each number shows how many mines touch that square. Flag the mines and uncover every safe square to clear the board. Your first dig is always safe. Pick any of five board sizes, from Tiny to Expert.

Tip: a 1 touching only one hidden square means that square is a mine.

Pinball

Launch the ball and keep it alive with the flippers. Hit bumpers, slingshots, the spinner and drop targets, light the lanes to raise your multiplier, and sink the saucer three times for a 3-ball multiball with growing jackpots.

Tip: aim for the saucer on the left to start multiball.

Pong

Move your paddle to hit the ball back past the computer. The first to 5 points wins. Hitting the ball near the paddle edge sends it at a sharper angle, and you pick how sharp the computer plays.

Tip: aim for the edges to outrun the computer paddle.

Rush Taxi

Steer through traffic and drive past waving passengers in the outside lane to pick them up. Reach the 🏁 before their timer runs out. Every 30 seconds the traffic gets heavier and the timers get shorter.

Tip: get into the outside lane early.

Simon Says

Watch the colored pads light up in a sequence, then repeat it. Every round adds one more step and plays a little faster.

Tip: turn the sound on, since each pad has its own note.

Sky Crane

A crane swings a block on a rope. Release it at the right moment so it lands on your tower. Off-center drops make the tower sway, and perfect drops steady it. You can miss 3 times.

Tip: release when the block is at the end of its swing, where it moves slowest.

Sliding Puzzle

The board is a grid of numbered tiles with one empty space. Tap a tile in the same row or column as the gap and it slides over, pushing any tiles between them along. Put the tiles in order, left to right and top to bottom, with the gap in the bottom right corner. Tiles that are already in their home spot get a light ring. Every shuffle can be solved.

Tip: solve the top row first, then the left column, and keep shrinking the puzzle until only a 2×2 corner is left.

Snake

Steer the snake to eat the red food. Each bite makes you longer, and every 5 bites raises the level and the speed. From level 3, blocks appear on the board. Hitting a wall, a block, or your own tail ends the game.

Tip: move along the edges to leave open space in the middle.

Solitaire

Build each suit from Ace to King. In the columns, stack cards downward in alternating colors, and only Kings can fill an empty column. Tap a card to move it automatically, tap the deck to draw, and use Undo if you change your mind.

Tip: uncover face-down cards as early as you can.

Sudoku

Fill the 9×9 grid so every row, column and 3×3 box contains the numbers 1 to 9 once. Choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert, and get a fresh puzzle every time. Use notes to pencil in options. Three mistakes ends the game.

Tip: start with the rows, columns and boxes that are nearly full.

Tic-Tac-Toe

Get three in a row before your opponent. Play the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard, where Hard never loses, or pick 2 Players to take turns with a friend.

Tip: take the center or a corner on your first move.

Tower Block

A block slides back and forth above your tower. Drop it so it lines up with the block below. Any overhang is cut off, so blocks get narrower. Perfect drops keep them wide.

Tip: watch the edge of the block, not the middle.

Tower of Hanoi

All the discs start stacked on the left peg, biggest at the bottom. Move them one at a time onto another peg, and never place a disc on top of a smaller one. Get the whole tower onto peg 3 to win. Every extra disc doubles the work: 3 discs take at least 7 moves, and 8 discs take at least 255.

Tip: move the smallest disc every other turn, always in the same direction around the pegs, and make the only other legal move in between.

Water Sort

Each tube holds four layers of colored water. Tap a tube to lift it, then tap another tube to pour into it. Water only pours onto the same color or into an empty tube, and all the matching layers on top pour together as long as there is room. A tube that is full of one color gets a cork. Sort every color to solve the puzzle and move on to the next one.

Tip: keep an empty tube free for as long as you can, it is your most useful tool.

Whack-a-Mole

Moles pop out of holes for a moment. Whack each one before it hides. Miss three and the game is over. The moles get faster the longer you last, and bombs cost a life too.

Tip: keep your eyes on the center hole and react outward.

Wood Blocks

You get three wooden pieces at a time in the tray below the board. Drag each one onto the 9×9 board wherever it fits; a green shadow shows where it will land. Filling a whole row, a whole column or one of the nine 3×3 boxes clears it and frees up space. When all three pieces are placed you get a new set. The game ends when none of the pieces you have left fits anywhere on the board.

Tip: every block you place scores a point, but clears are where the big points are: clearing two or three lines with one piece multiplies the bonus, and clearing again with the next piece keeps a streak going.

Word Finder

Each puzzle has a theme, like Animals, Space or Music, and a list of words hidden in the grid. Find a word and drag across it from its first letter to its last in a straight line. Found words get a colored highlight and are crossed off the list. Easy hides 6 words across and down on an 8 by 8 grid, Medium hides 9 on a 10 by 10 grid with diagonals, and Hard hides 12 on a 12 by 12 grid in every direction, including backwards.

Tip: scan for uncommon letters like K, W, Y or Z first, since they stand out in the grid.

Word Guess

Type any real word with the right number of letters and press Enter. Each tile then flips: green means the letter is in the right spot, yellow means it is in the word but somewhere else, and grey means it is not in the word at all. If a letter appears twice in your guess but only once in the answer, only one tile lights up. Use the clues to narrow it down and find the word within six guesses. When a game ends, tap Next word for a fresh one.

Tip: start with a word that has several common letters, like vowels and R, S, T or N.
