How to play

Pocket Arcade is a collection of free classic games that run directly in your web browser. There is nothing to download or install, and every game works with a touch screen, a mouse, or a keyboard. Here is a quick guide to each one.

2048

Slide all tiles in one direction. Matching numbers merge into one tile with double the value. Reach bigger tiles to clear each goal, from 128 upward. The game ends when no moves are left.

Tip: keep your biggest tile in one corner.

Blob Maze

Guide the blob through the maze to eat every dot while avoiding the spiky enemies. Eat a gem and the enemies get scared for a few seconds, so you can eat them for bonus points. Side tunnels wrap around the screen.

Tip: save gems for when enemies are close.

Breakout

Bounce the ball off your paddle to break every brick. Some bricks need two or three hits. You have 3 lives, and each mode has its own brick layout.

Tip: hit the ball with the paddle edge to aim it.

Brick Balls

Drag to aim and fire a stream of balls at numbered bricks. Each hit lowers a brick by one. The bricks move down after every shot, and the game ends when one reaches the floor. Collect +1 rings for extra balls.

Tip: bank shots off the walls to hit bricks from the side.

Bubble Pop

Shoot bouncing bubbles to split them until they pop, before the time bar runs out. Levels never end, some are split by walls, and power-ups like the sticky harpoon and dynamite help out. Two players can share one keyboard.

Tip: split big bubbles while they are high above you.

Cannon Blast

Drag your cannon while it fires nonstop at bouncing rocks. Each rock splits in two when its number hits zero, and they keep getting tougher the longer you last. Grab the occasional power-up, and don't let a rock touch you.

Tip: keep moving and stay out from under falling rocks.

Chess

Play White against the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard, or pick 2 Players to play a friend on one device. Castling, en passant and promotion are all included.

Tip: control the center and castle early.

Flappy Bird

Tap to flap and keep the bird in the air. Fly through the gaps between pipes without touching them. Every 8 pipes the level goes up and the gaps get narrower.

Tip: use small, steady taps rather than big jumps.

Fuse Jumper

Leap and glide around the screen to collect all 24 bombs. Grab the live bomb for double points before its countdown runs out and it explodes, and follow the live bombs in order for a big end-of-level bonus. Dodge the mummies and birds, or grab the P ball to turn them into coins.

Tip: hold jump while falling to glide past enemies.

Hangman

Guess the hidden word one letter at a time before six wrong guesses complete the drawing. Words get longer on each level, or tap 2 players and type a secret word for a friend.

Tip: start with common vowels and letters like R, S, T and N.

Helix Drop

Spin the tower so the bouncing ball drops through the gaps in each ring. Red sections end your run. Fall through three rings in a row to smash the next one, and see how deep you can go. The tower never ends.

Tip: line up the next gap while the ball is still in the air.

Land Grab

Leave your land to draw a trail, then loop back to claim the trail and everything inside it. If a rival crosses your trail you are out, but you can knock them out the same way. Claim enough of the map to win.

Tip: short loops keep your trail safe.

Memory Match

All cards start face down. Flip two at a time to find matching pairs. Pick from 6 pairs up to 15. Fewer moves means a better score.

Tip: flip unknown cards first so you remember more positions.

Minesweeper

Dig squares to uncover the board. Each number shows how many mines touch that square. Flag the mines and uncover every safe square to clear the board. Your first dig is always safe. Pick any of five board sizes, from Tiny to Expert.

Tip: a 1 touching only one hidden square means that square is a mine.

Pinball

Launch the ball and keep it alive with the flippers. Hit bumpers, slingshots, the spinner and drop targets, light the lanes to raise your multiplier, and sink the saucer three times for a 3-ball multiball with growing jackpots.

Tip: aim for the saucer on the left to start multiball.

Pong

Move your paddle to hit the ball back past the computer. The first to 5 points wins. Hitting the ball near the paddle edge sends it at a sharper angle, and you pick how sharp the computer plays.

Tip: aim for the edges to outrun the computer paddle.

Rush Taxi

Steer through traffic and drive past waving passengers in the outside lane to pick them up. Reach the 🏁 before their timer runs out. Every 30 seconds the traffic gets heavier and the timers get shorter.

Tip: get into the outside lane early.

Simon Says

Watch the colored pads light up in a sequence, then repeat it. Every round adds one more step and plays a little faster.

Tip: turn the sound on, since each pad has its own note.

Sky Crane

A crane swings a block on a rope. Release it at the right moment so it lands on your tower. Off-center drops make the tower sway, and perfect drops steady it. You can miss 3 times.

Tip: release when the block is at the end of its swing, where it moves slowest.

Snake

Steer the snake to eat the red food. Each bite makes you longer, and every 5 bites raises the level and the speed. From level 3, blocks appear on the board. Hitting a wall, a block, or your own tail ends the game.

Tip: move along the edges to leave open space in the middle.

Solitaire

Build each suit from Ace to King. In the columns, stack cards downward in alternating colors, and only Kings can fill an empty column. Tap a card to move it automatically, tap the deck to draw, and use Undo if you change your mind.

Tip: uncover face-down cards as early as you can.

Sudoku

Fill the 9×9 grid so every row, column and 3×3 box contains the numbers 1 to 9 once. Choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert, and get a fresh puzzle every time. Use notes to pencil in options. Three mistakes ends the game.

Tip: start with the rows, columns and boxes that are nearly full.

Tic-Tac-Toe

Get three in a row before your opponent. Play the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard, where Hard never loses, or pick 2 Players to take turns with a friend.

Tip: take the center or a corner on your first move.

Tower Block

A block slides back and forth above your tower. Drop it so it lines up with the block below. Any overhang is cut off, so blocks get narrower. Perfect drops keep them wide.

Tip: watch the edge of the block, not the middle.

Whack-a-Mole

Moles pop out of holes for a moment. Whack each one before it hides. Miss three and the game is over. The moles get faster the longer you last, and bombs cost a life too.

Tip: keep your eyes on the center hole and react outward.
