How to play Block Fill

Every board has one glowing start tile, some open tiles and a few blocked ones. Starting from the glowing tile, draw a path that moves up, down, left or right, one tile at a time. The path can never visit a tile twice and can't pass through blocked tiles. Fill every open tile to solve the puzzle. If you get stuck, drag back along your path to undo, or touch any filled tile to cut the path back to it.

Controls

  • Phone: drag your finger across the tiles, or tap the next tile next to the snake's head.
  • Keyboard/mouse: drag with the mouse, or use the arrow keys or WASD to move the head. ↺ Clear starts over.

Tips

Tiles with only one open neighbor must be the last tile of your path, so plan to finish there. Avoid cutting the board into two separate areas, since you can't jump between them. Corners and spots next to blocked tiles are best filled early. Every board is freshly made and always has a solution, and your solved count for each difficulty is saved in your browser.
