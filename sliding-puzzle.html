How to play Sliding Puzzle

The board is a grid of numbered tiles with one empty space. Tap a tile in the same row or column as the gap and it slides over, pushing any tiles between them along. Put the tiles in order, left to right and top to bottom, with the gap in the bottom right corner. Tiles that are already in their home spot get a light ring. Every shuffle can be solved.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a tile in the gap's row or column to slide it, or swipe on the board to move a tile into the gap.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click a tile, or press the arrow keys to move a tile into the gap in that direction.

Tips

Solve the top row first, then the left column, and keep shrinking the puzzle until only a 2×2 corner is left. Put the last two tiles of a row in place together by parking them in the row below first. Tiles are colored by the row they belong in. Your best time and fewest moves for each size are saved in the browser.
