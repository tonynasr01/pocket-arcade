How to play Simon Says

Watch the colored pads light up in a sequence, then repeat it. Every round adds one more step and plays a little faster.

Controls

Watch the pads light up, then tap them in the same order. Sound on helps.

Tips

Turn the sound on, since each pad has its own note.
