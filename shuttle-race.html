How to play Shuttle Race

Each player has 5 pieces in 5 lanes, and the two sets of lanes cross in the middle. On your turn, move one piece forward exactly as many spaces as the dots at the start of its leg show: 1, 2 or 3. A piece that reaches the far edge stops there, turns around and comes back at its return speed. If your piece runs into enemy pieces, it jumps over them and stops on the first free space after them, and every piece it jumped goes back to the start of its current leg. The first player to bring 4 of 5 pieces home wins.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a piece, or anywhere along its lane, to move it.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click a piece to move it; hovering shows where it will land. Keys 1–5 move a piece, or pick one with the arrow keys and press Enter.

Tips

Fast pieces are fast only one way: a 3 going out is a 1 coming back. Jumping a piece that is far along its leg costs your rival a lot of turns. Avoid stopping right in front of an enemy piece that is about to move, and use slow pieces to block the lanes your rival needs.
