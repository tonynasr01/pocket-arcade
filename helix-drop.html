How to play Helix Drop

The ball bounces on rings around a tall tower. Spin the tower to line a gap up under the ball so it drops to the next ring. Landing on a red section ends the run. Fall through three rings in a row and the ball smashes straight through the next ring, red or not. The tower never ends: every ring you drop through scores points, chains of drops score more, and the rings get trickier the deeper you go.

Controls

  • Drag sideways on the tower to spin it.
  • Keyboard: ← → to spin.

Tips

Spin early while the ball is in the air. Every 10 rings brings more red sections and narrower gaps. Your best score is saved in your browser.
