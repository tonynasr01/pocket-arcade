How to play Jean Escapes the Toilet

Each chapter locks Jean in a different toilet: his bathroom at home, the office restroom and a tiny airplane lavatory. Turn between the four walls with the arrows and tap anything to inspect it; Jean has something to say about everything. Useful things go into the inventory bar. Select an item and tap a spot in the room to use it there, or tap a second item to combine the two. Clues hidden in the room give you the codes for padlocks and keypads, and every puzzle can be solved from what you find.

Controls

  • Phone: tap objects to inspect them, tap an item to select it and tap it again to look at it closely. Use ◀ ▶ to turn and ▼ Back to leave a close-up.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click objects and items. Left and right arrows turn, Down or Escape goes back, keys 1-6 select items and H shows a hint.

Tips

Read everything you pick up: notes and tickets often hold the code you need. If you are stuck, the 💡 button gives a gentle nudge first and the exact answer if you tap it again. Your progress in each chapter is saved in your browser, so you can close the game and carry on later, and ↺ starts the chapter over.
