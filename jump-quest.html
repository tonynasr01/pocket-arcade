How to play Jump Quest

Guide your little sprout hero from the start of each level to the flag pole at the end. Jump on walkers, hoppers and flyers to knock them out, and stomp a shell beetle to tuck it in its shell, then kick the shell to bowl over other enemies. Bump the shining blocks from below for coins and power-ups: a berry makes you bigger so you can take one extra hit and smash bricks, and a spark flower lets you throw bouncing sparks. Pits, spikes and lava cost a life, but checkpoint flags save your progress. Clear a level to unlock the next one.

Controls

  • Phone: slide your thumb on the left half of the game to run, tap the right half to jump (hold for a higher jump), and swipe down on the right half to throw sparks. You can switch to a wheel or arrow buttons under the game.
  • Keyboard: ← → or A D to run, Space, ↑, W or Z to jump, and X or Shift to throw sparks.

Tips

Hold jump for a big leap and tap it for a small hop. You can still jump a split second after running off a ledge, and a jump pressed just before landing still counts. Hold jump while bouncing off an enemy to fly higher. Touch the flag pole as high as you can for up to 5,000 bonus points, and every 50 coins gives an extra life. Your best score for each level is saved in the browser.
