How to play Rock Paper Scissors

Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, and paper beats rock. The first player to win five rounds takes the match. In Mind Reader mode the computer studies the order of your moves and tries to predict the next one.

Controls

Pick a move. Mind reader mode learns your patterns.

Tips

Against mind reader, avoid repeating patterns.
