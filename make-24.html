How to play Make 24

You get four number cards. Tap a card, tap an operation (+, −, × or ÷), then tap a second card: the two cards merge into one showing the result. Repeat until only one card is left. If it shows 24, you solved it and a new puzzle is dealt. Every card must be used exactly once. Results can be negative or fractions, which are shown as stacked fractions and can be used in later steps. Every puzzle has at least one solution. Hard puzzles often need a fraction along the way, and Target mode asks for a random number from 10 to 50 with four or five cards.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a card, an operation and another card. Tap a selected card again to drop it. Undo, Reset, Hint and Skip sit under the operations.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click the cards, or press 1 to 5 to pick a card and + − * / for the operations. U or Backspace undoes, R resets, H gives a hint, S skips, Esc clears your selection.

Tips

Look for pairs that make 24 easily: 3 × 8, 4 × 6, 2 × 12 or 20 + 4, and try to build the missing factor from the other cards. When nothing works with whole numbers, try a division that leaves a fraction, as in 8 ÷ (3 − 8 ÷ 3). Hint shows one first step that still leads to a solution. Skipping shows a solution but resets your streak. Your solved count, current streak and best streak are saved in the browser for each mode.
