How to play Binary Bits

Each card is one bit, and each bit is worth double the one to its right: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 and 128. When a number is shown, switch on the cards that add up to it. When bits are shown, work out their value and type it on the number pad. Hex rounds pack four bits into each digit, from 0 to 9 and then A (10) to F (15). Every correct answer speeds up the clock, and three misses end the game.

Controls

  • Phone: tap the bit cards to switch them on or off, and tap the number pad to type an answer, then ✓.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click cards or press 1 to 8 to flip them from left to right. Type digits (or A to F in hex rounds), Backspace to fix, Enter to check.

Tips

Start from the biggest card: if the number is at least its value, switch it on and take that value away, then move right. To read bits, add up only the lit cards. For hex, split the byte into two groups of four and convert each group on its own. Fast answers and long streaks score more, and your best score for each mode is saved in the browser.
