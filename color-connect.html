How to play Color Connect

Each board has pairs of colored dots. Drag from one dot to its twin to draw a pipe through the squares in between. Pipes can only turn at right angles and can never cross each other. If you drag through another pipe, it gets cut back, and it grows again if you pull your pipe away during the same drag. A puzzle is solved when every pair is connected and no empty square is left.

Controls

  • Phone: drag with your finger from a dot to its match. Touch the middle of a pipe to keep drawing from there.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click and drag from a dot to its match. Press ↻ or Next puzzle for a new board.

Tips

Start with pairs near the edges and corners, since they have the fewest ways to go. Pipes that hug the walls often leave room for the rest. If a square is left empty, look for a pipe that can take a detour through it. Every board is freshly made and always has a solution, and the number of puzzles you solve at each size is saved in your browser.
