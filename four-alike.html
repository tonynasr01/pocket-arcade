How to play Four Alike

There are 16 different pieces. Each one is tall or short, light or dark, round or square, and hollow or solid. On your turn you place the piece your opponent chose for you on any empty square, then you pick one of the remaining pieces for your opponent to place. Make a line of four pieces (a row, a column or a diagonal) that all share at least one trait, such as four tall or four hollow pieces, and you win. Pieces belong to nobody, so the player who completes the line wins, whoever placed the others. If the board fills with no matching line, the game is a draw.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a piece in the tray, then tap Give (or tap it again) to hand it over. Tap an empty square to place the piece you were given.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click the same way, or use the arrow keys to move around the tray or the board and press Enter to choose.

Tips

Before you give a piece, check every line with three pieces in it: if the piece you hand over matches a trait they share, your opponent wins. Late in the game, try to leave a board where every remaining piece is dangerous, so your opponent has to give you a winner. Medium never hands you a winning piece when it can avoid it, and Hard looks several turns ahead.
