How to play 30 Seconds Rush

Split into 2 to 4 teams, name them and choose a target score or a number of rounds. On each turn one player is the describer: pass them the phone, tap Ready and five words appear with a 30 second clock. Describe each word without saying it, spelling it or using part of it, while your team shouts out guesses. Tap a word as soon as it is guessed, and tap it again if you made a mistake. Every guessed word is a point, and the first team past the target at the end of a round wins.

Controls

  • Phone: tap Ready to start the clock, tap each word when your team guesses it, and tap Done early if you are finished.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click the words or press 1 to 5 to mark them, and press Enter to start, finish or move on.

Tips

Use quick clues like rhymes, opposites and famous examples instead of long sentences, and skip a hard word and come back to it. Turn on the dice option for the classic twist where a roll of 0 to 2 takes words off your card. Pick categories to suit your group, or play Kids mode with easier words. Your teams and settings are saved in this browser.
