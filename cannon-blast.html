How to play Cannon Blast

Your cannon fires a nonstop stream of shots straight up. Each rock shows how many hits it can take. When it reaches zero it breaks into two smaller rocks with half as many hits, and the smallest ones break for good. There are no levels: rocks keep coming, and the longer you last, the tougher and more numerous they get. If a rock touches your cannon, the game is over.

Controls

  • Touch anywhere on the game and the cannon jumps right under your finger, then follows it as you slide.
  • Keyboard: ← → to move, Space to start.
  • The cannon fires on its own.

Tips

Keep moving so bouncing rocks never land on you. About every 20 seconds a broken rock drops a glowing gold UPGRADE. Upgrades last for the whole run, so never miss one: ➕ adds a barrel (up to 3), ⏩ fires faster (6 levels) and 💪 hits harder (8 levels). Your upgrade levels show along the ground. Every so often a broken rock also drops a short power-up: drive under it to grab it. ⚡ doubles your fire rate, 🔱 adds two angled streams, 💥 doubles your power, ❄️ freezes the rocks, and 🛡️ blocks one hit. Your best score is saved in your browser.
