How to play Wall Race

You start in the middle of the bottom row and win by reaching any square of the top row before your rival reaches the bottom. On each turn you either move your pawn one square up, down, left or right, or place one wall. A wall is two squares long and blocks movement through the gap it sits in. Each player has 10 walls, walls may not overlap or cross, and you can never close off the last way through for either player. When the two pawns meet face to face you may jump straight over, or step to the side if a wall is behind your rival.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a glowing square to move. Tap a gap between squares to preview a wall, tap the same spot again or ⟲ Turn to rotate it, then tap ✓ Place.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click squares and gaps, or use the arrow keys to move. Press W for a wall, arrows to slide it, R to turn it and Enter to place it.

Tips

Don't spend your walls too early: a wall is strongest when it forces a long detour right in front of your rival. Keep an eye on your own shortest way to the goal so a single wall can't push you far back. Walls placed side by side can build long corridors that lead the other pawn away from its goal. Hard mode looks a few turns ahead, so try Medium first.
