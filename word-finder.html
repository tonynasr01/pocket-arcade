How to play Word Finder

Each puzzle has a theme, like Animals, Space or Music, and a list of words hidden in the grid. Find a word and drag across it from its first letter to its last in a straight line. Found words get a colored highlight and are crossed off the list. Easy hides 6 words across and down on an 8 by 8 grid, Medium hides 9 on a 10 by 10 grid with diagonals, and Hard hides 12 on a 12 by 12 grid in every direction, including backwards.

Controls

  • Phone: drag your finger across a word, or tap its first letter and then its last.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click and drag from the first letter to the last, or click the first letter and then the last.

Tips

Scan for uncommon letters like K, W, Y or Z first, since they stand out in the grid. On Hard, remember words can run backwards and upwards. Your best time for each difficulty is saved in this browser, and New puzzle deals a fresh theme any time.
