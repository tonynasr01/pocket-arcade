How to play Memory Match

All cards start face down. Flip two at a time to find matching pairs. Pick how many cards to play with, from 6 pairs up to 15. Fewer moves means a better score.

Controls

Tap two cards. Matching pairs stay face up. Choose the number of pairs with the menu at the top.

Tips

Flip unknown cards first so you remember more positions.

Your best score is saved in your browser.
