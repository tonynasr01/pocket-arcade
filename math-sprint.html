How to play Math Sprint

Pick a mode at the top: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division, Mixed or Hard mix, then press Start. A sum appears and you type the answer. There is no Enter key to press: as soon as you have typed as many digits as the answer has, it is marked right or wrong and the next sum appears. Every right answer scores points, and a streak of 5 in a row doubles them (10 triples, 15 quadruples) and adds 3 seconds to the clock. A wrong answer shakes, shows the right result and resets your streak. Hard mix brings bigger numbers, negative numbers and squares.

Controls

  • Phone: tap the big number pad under the sum. ⌫ deletes a digit and C clears. In Hard mix, press − before the digits for a negative answer.
  • Keyboard/mouse: type digits on the keyboard (or click the pad). Minus (typed first) makes the answer negative, Backspace deletes, Esc clears, Enter or Space starts a new round.

Tips

Speed comes from streaks: a steady run of right answers is worth far more than rushing and breaking your streak. For times tables, learn the squares (6 × 6, 7 × 7, 8 × 8) and work out neighbours from them. For a negative answer in Hard mix, press − first: the answer is checked as soon as the digits are in. Your best score for each mode is saved in the browser.
