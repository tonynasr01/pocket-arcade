How to play Water Sort

Each tube holds four layers of colored water. Tap a tube to lift it, then tap another tube to pour into it. Water only pours onto the same color or into an empty tube, and all the matching layers on top pour together as long as there is room. A tube that is full of one color gets a cork. Sort every color to solve the puzzle and move on to the next one.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a tube to lift it, tap another tube to pour. Use Undo and + Tube under the board.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click tubes, or press 1–9 and 0 to pick tubes by number. Z or Backspace undoes a pour, Esc puts a lifted tube back.

Tips

Keep an empty tube free for as long as you can, it is your most useful tool. Try to finish one color at a time instead of moving water back and forth. Every puzzle can be solved, and Undo is unlimited, so experiment. If you get really stuck, + Tube gives you one extra empty tube per puzzle. Your puzzle number for each size is saved in the browser.
