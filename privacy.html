This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit Pocket Arcade at pocket-arcade.games (the "Site"), and how it is used. By using the Site, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Pocket Arcade does not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or other personal details to play.

Game progress stored on your device. Your unlocked levels and best scores are saved in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Information you send us. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply.

To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, through a consent message provided by Google. You can change your choices at any time through the privacy settings link shown on the Site.

Other third-party services

The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, for security and performance.

Children's privacy

The Site is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not store personal information about players, most requests can be handled by clearing your browser data and adjusting your ad settings above. For anything else, contact us.
