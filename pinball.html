How to play Pinball

Pull the plunger to launch the ball, then keep it in play with the flippers. Bumpers and slingshots score points and send the ball flying. Roll through the three top lanes to raise your multiplier up to ×5, knock down the three drop targets for 5,000, and shoot through the spinner on the left for points on every spin. Sink the saucer three times to start a 3-ball multiball, then sink it again for jackpots that grow each time. Light both side lanes for 20 seconds of super bumpers, and whip the ball around the top for an orbit bonus. You get three balls, a ball save on each one, and an extra ball at 50,000 points.

Controls

  • Phone: tap and hold the left or right side of the table to raise that flipper. To launch, hold anywhere and let go.
  • Keyboard: ← → or Z and M for the flippers. Hold Space or ↓ to pull the plunger and release to launch.
  • The longer you hold the plunger, the harder the launch.

Tips

Hold a flipper up to catch the ball, then aim. The saucer on the left upper side is the key shot: three sinks start multiball, and during multiball every sink is a jackpot. Watch the display at the top for what to shoot next. If the ball ever gets stuck, the table nudges it. Your best score is saved in your browser.
