How to play Wahoo

Each player has four marbles in a start nest and a four-hole home row in the middle of their arm of the cross. Roll a 1 or a 6 to bring a marble out onto your start hole (the ringed hole beside your nest); a 6 also gives you another roll. Marbles move clockwise exactly the number rolled, and you may not pass or land on one of your own marbles. Landing exactly on a rival's marble sends it back to its nest. After nearly a full lap your marbles turn into your home row at your arm's tip, which needs exact rolls and cannot be jumped inside. Shortcut: a move that reaches one of the four inner corner holes with exactly one step left may step into the center hole, and a marble in the center leaves only on a roll of 1, onto any corner hole. The first player to fill all four home holes wins.

Controls

  • Phone: tap Roll or the board to roll, then tap a glowing marble. If a marble has two choices, tap the highlighted hole you want.
  • Keyboard/mouse: Space or Enter rolls; click a glowing marble, or use the arrow keys or Tab to cycle the possible moves and Enter to play one. Keys 1 to 4 pick a marble.

Tips

Bring marbles out early when you roll a 1 or 6, but do not crowd your start hole. Try not to stop one to six holes in front of a rival, since that is exactly where they can land on you, and look for chances to hit marbles that have travelled far. The center is a big shortcut, but you can sit there a while waiting for a 1, and a rival can land on you there too.
