How to play Wood Blocks

You get three wooden pieces at a time in the tray below the board. Drag each one onto the 9×9 board wherever it fits; a green shadow shows where it will land. Filling a whole row, a whole column or one of the nine 3×3 boxes clears it and frees up space. When all three pieces are placed you get a new set. The game ends when none of the pieces you have left fits anywhere on the board.

Controls

  • Phone: touch a piece in the tray and drag it onto the board. The piece floats above your finger so you can see where it goes.
  • Keyboard/mouse: drag pieces with the mouse, or press 1, 2 or 3 to pick a piece, move it with the arrow keys and press Enter to place it (Esc to cancel).

Tips

Every block you place scores a point, but clears are where the big points are: clearing two or three lines with one piece multiplies the bonus, and clearing again with the next piece keeps a streak going. Keep an open space for the big 3×3 square and the long 5-block lines. Pieces that can no longer fit are greyed out. Your best score is saved in your browser.
