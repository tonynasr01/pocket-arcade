How to play Backgammon

Each side has 15 checkers. You play White and move counter-clockwise toward your home board in the bottom right, while Red moves the opposite way. Roll two dice and move one checker by each number, or one checker by both; doubles are played four times, and you must use both dice when you can. A single checker on a point is a blot: land on it to hit it and send it to the bar, where it must re-enter in your opponent's home board before anything else moves. Once all 15 of your checkers are home you can bear them off, and the first side to bear off every checker wins.

Controls

  • Phone: tap Roll (or the middle of the board), tap a checker, then tap a highlighted point or the tray to bear off. Tap Done to finish your turn.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click checkers and highlighted points. Space or Enter rolls and ends the turn, U or Backspace undoes a move.

Tips

Avoid leaving blots where your opponent has many shots at them, and build points side by side to form a prime that traps their back checkers. Making your 5 and 4 points early is strong. Watch the pip counts in the tray: when you are well ahead in the race, break contact and run for home.
