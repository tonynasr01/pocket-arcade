How to play Shape Grid

The board has 4 rows, 4 columns and four 2×2 zones. Each player has 8 pieces: two circles, two squares, two triangles and two diamonds. On your turn place one piece on any empty square, but never in a row, column or zone where your opponent already has the same shape (your own matching shape is fine). The first player to complete a row, column or zone holding all four different shapes wins, no matter whose colors they are. If it is your turn and you have no legal move, you lose.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a shape in your tray, then tap one of the squares marked with a dot.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click a shape and a square, or press 1–4 to choose a shape, move with the arrow keys and press Enter to place.

Tips

Placing the third different shape in a line is risky: it invites your opponent to finish it with the fourth. Use your pieces to block shapes, since every shape you put down forbids that shape to your opponent across its row, column and zone. Toward the end, count which moves remain so the other side runs out first. Hard mode searches deep, so it rarely misses a trap.
