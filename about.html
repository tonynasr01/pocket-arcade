About Pocket Arcade

Pocket Arcade is a small collection of free games that run right in your web browser. There is nothing to download, no account to create, and no waiting. Open the page on your phone, tablet, or computer and start playing.

61 games, one page. The collection includes classics like Chess, Checkers, Backgammon, Billiards, Solitaire, Color Clash, Color Clash Flip, Snake, Pong, Breakout, 2048, Memory Match, Hangman, Minesweeper, Sudoku, Four in a Row, Dots and Boxes and Tic-Tac-Toe, puzzles such as Water Sort, Tower of Hanoi, Sliding Puzzle, Color Connect, Block Fill, Wood Blocks, Word Guess and Word Finder, along with arcade-style games such as Flappy Bird, Tower Block, Sky Crane, Blob Maze, Rush Taxi, Land Grab, Helix Drop, Brick Balls, Bubble Pop, Cannon Blast, Fuse Jumper, and Pinball.

Built to be light and fast

Every game is written in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no images or heavy libraries. The whole site is smaller than a single photo on most websites, so it loads quickly even on slow mobile connections.

Levels and progress

Most games have levels that unlock as you play, and many keep track of your best score. Your progress is saved privately in your own browser, not on our servers.

Free to play

Pocket Arcade is supported by advertising, which keeps every game free. If you have an idea for a new game or find a bug, we would love to hear from you on the contact page.
