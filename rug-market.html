How to play Rug Market

Each turn you may turn the merchant a quarter turn left or right (never backward), then roll the special die (1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4) and he walks that many squares forward. When he walks off the edge he follows the curved loop back onto the neighbouring row or column, and the corner loops turn him around the corner. If he stops on a rival's rug you pay that player one coin for every square of the connected patch of their color that he stands on. Then you lay one of your rugs, two squares long, with at least one half right next to the merchant: never under him, and never exactly on top of one whole rug. When all rugs are laid, coins plus visible rug squares decide the winner, and anyone who runs out of coins is out.

Controls

  • Phone: tap ⟲, ↑ or ⟳ to choose where the merchant faces (red badges show tolls you might pay), then tap Roll. Tap a square next to the merchant to preview your rug, tap its other half, tap again or use ⟳ Turn to rotate, then tap ✓ Place.
  • Keyboard/mouse: Left and Right turn the merchant, Up goes straight, Space or Enter rolls. When placing, arrow keys cycle through every legal rug spot, R rotates and Enter places. You can also click squares.

Tips

Big connected patches of your color are worth the most: rivals who land there pay for every square. Cover the middle of a rival's large patch to split it in two, and steer the merchant away from expensive patches by checking the badges before you roll. A 2 or 3 is twice as likely as a 1 or a 4.
