How to play Ball Pyramid

Each player has 15 balls. On your turn, place a ball from your reserve on any free spot of the 4×4 base, or on a spot resting on four balls. Instead, you can move one of your own balls that holds nothing up to a higher spot, which saves a ball from your reserve. When your ball completes a 2×2 square of your color on any level, you may take back 1 or 2 of your free balls. The player who places the ball on top of the pyramid wins, and a player with no balls left loses.

Controls

  • Phone: tap a dashed spot to place a ball. Tap one of your balls and then a gold spot to move it up. After a square, tap 1 or 2 balls to take back, or tap Keep all.
  • Keyboard/mouse: click spots and balls the same way. Esc cancels a selected ball and Enter finishes taking balls back.

Tips

Every ball you save counts: the player with more balls at the end gets to place the top. Moving a ball up is like a free turn because it does not use your reserve. Block squares where your opponent already has three balls, and take back balls that let you make the same square again.
