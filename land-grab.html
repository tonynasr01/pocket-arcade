How to play Land Grab

You start with a small square of land. Move out of it to leave a trail, then return to your land to claim the trail and everything it encloses. If a rival crosses your trail, or you run into your own trail or the wall, you are out. Cross a rival's trail to knock them out. Claim enough of the map to clear each level.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or WASD on a computer.
  • Swipe anywhere on the game, or use the joystick, on a phone.
  • You can't turn straight back on yourself.

Tips

Short, quick loops are safer than big ones, because your trail is only vulnerable while you are outside your land. Each level needs more land and adds more rivals.
