How to play Land Grab

You start with a small square of land. Move out of it to leave a trail, then return to your land to claim the trail and everything it encloses. If a rival crosses your trail, or you run into your own trail or the wall, you are out. Cross a rival's trail to knock them out. Claim enough of the map to win. Pick a mode from Easy to Master: harder modes need more land and bring more rivals.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or WASD on a computer.
  • Swipe anywhere on the game, or use the wheel or arrow buttons, on a phone (switch between them under the controls).
  • You can't turn straight back on yourself.

Tips

Short, quick loops are safer than big ones, because your trail is only vulnerable while you are outside your land. Harder modes need more land and add more rivals.
