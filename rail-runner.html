How to play Rail Runner

You run along three train tracks. Switch tracks to avoid oncoming trains, jump over low striped barriers, and roll under high signs marked ROLL. Coins are worth 10 points each, and every meter you run adds a point. The run gets faster over time.

Controls

  • Swipe left or right to switch tracks.
  • Swipe up to jump and down to roll.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD, and Space to jump.

Tips

Watch the far end of the tracks so you can plan your lane early. Swiping down in the air drops you to the ground fast.
